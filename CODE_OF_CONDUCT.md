# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Everyone participating in the FormSummaryBundle community is expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project discussions.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Giving technical feedback about code and ideas rather than about people.
- Welcoming questions and contributors with different backgrounds or experience levels.
- Respecting differing opinions and correcting mistakes constructively.
- Keeping discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **alkinbg@gmail.com**. Include the relevant links and context, but do not post sensitive details publicly.

The maintainer may moderate discussions, remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation, or block accounts when necessary. Reports will be reviewed with discretion, and information will be shared only as needed to address the concern.

This policy applies to project-maintained spaces. GitHub's own terms and community policies also apply.
